## Rules

- Use real ALPA screenshots naturally integrated into environments.
- Do not invent fake dashboards when real assets are available.
- Keep branding subtle and believable.
- Avoid oversized logos or unrealistic UI overlays.
- Prefer cinematic realism over futuristic SaaS aesthetics.
- Screens should feel operational, not promotional.
