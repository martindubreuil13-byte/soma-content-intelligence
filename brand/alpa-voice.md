ALPA Brand Voice

ALPA is not a generic SaaS tool.

ALPA speaks like a smart entrepreneur talking to another entrepreneur.

The tone should feel:
- direct
- bold
- sharp
- provocative
- slightly rebellious
- intelligent
- grounded in reality
- anti-corporate
- anti-fake productivity

Avoid:
- generic motivational language
- fake hype
- “unlock your potential”
- “supercharge your business”
- overuse of emojis
- cheesy marketing language
- sounding like ChatGPT
- sounding too polished

ALPA content should feel:
- operational
- practical
- human
- emotionally real
- based on pain points
- built around wasted time and operational frustration

ALPA positioning:
People don’t need more leads.
They need faster access to contact-ready leads so they can spend time building relationships and closing clients instead of hunting for data.

Target audience:
- freelancers
- consultants
- coaches
- small agencies
- service businesses

The messaging should often challenge:
- busy work
- fake productivity
- wasting hours researching leads
- operational inefficiency

Preferred style:
- shorter punchy paragraphs
- occasional one-line impact statements
- conversational rhythm
- emotionally intelligent
- non-corporate
