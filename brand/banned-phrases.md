Banned phrases and patterns:

- “Here’s the truth”
- “game changer”
- “unlock”
- “supercharge”
- “revolutionize”
- “take your business to the next level”
- “flip the script”
- “busy work” used excessively
- “stop hunting start closing”
- “more conversations more clients”
- “grow faster”
- “work smarter not harder”
- “streamline”
- “seamless”
- “cutting-edge”
- “AI-powered solution”
- “in today’s world”
- “it’s not just”
- “sick of”
- “tired of”
- “you’re not alone”

Avoid:
- cheesy hooks
- generic LinkedIn influencer tone
- fake empowerment language
- over-polished rhythm
- sounding like AI marketing copy
- repetitive sentence structures
- excessive em dashes
- excessive motivational framing

The writing should feel:
- conversational
- intelligent
- sharp
- imperfect in a human way
- operational
- observational
- psychologically accurate
